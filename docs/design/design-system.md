# Sai Nath University — Design System & UI Specifications

## 1. Visual Identity & Brand Philosophy

- **Institutional Creed:** Academic Excellence, Regional Empowerment, Rigorous Professional Competence.
- **Brand Colors:**
  - **Royal Maroon / Crimson (`#8B1E28`):** Dominant institutional heritage color symbolizing dignity, perseverance, and academic authority.
  - **Academic Navy (`#1B365D`):** Secondary authority color representing integrity, engineering precision, and scholarly tradition.
  - **University Gold (`#C89D3C`):** Accent color highlighting key calls to action, honors, and verified statutory approvals.
  - **Background Surfaces:** Pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) and ultra-light warm gray (`#F8FAFC`). No dark backgrounds for general content sections.

## 2. Typography Hierarchy

- **Headings (Display / Editorial):** System serif or clean high-legibility sans-serif with generous letter spacing and strong visual hierarchy.
- **Body & Controls:** Modern accessible sans-serif (`font-sans`), optimized for readability with minimum 16px base font size on mobile devices.
- **Contrast Ratios:** All text elements adhere strictly to WCAG 2.2 AA standards (minimum 4.5:1 contrast against background).

## 3. UI Component Roster

1. **`UniversityHeader`:** Multi-tier header featuring statutory recognition banner (UGC, AIU, BCI, PCI, INC), student corner quick links, emergency contact lines, and primary navigation bar.
2. **`NavigationMenu` & `MobileNavigation`:** Accessible keyboard-navigable desktop megamenu and responsive full-screen mobile drawer with focus trap.
3. **`HeroSection`:** High-impact institutional introduction, quick stats badge strip, admissions CTA, and video/campus showcase.
4. **`AnnouncementBanner`:** Sticky, pauseable marquee / ticker displaying urgent exam schedules, admission deadlines, and circulars.
5. **`ProgramCard` & `DepartmentCard`:** Clean cards with degree badges, eligibility highlights, duration, and curriculum download actions.
6. **`FacultyCard`:** Academic credential cards with specialization tags and contact links.
7. **`DocumentList`:** Filterable statutory document table with PDF icons, size badges, issue dates, and direct download buttons.
8. **`SearchBox` & `Breadcrumb`:** Instant client-side search across all programs and circulars; accessible breadcrumb navigation.
9. **`ApplicationStatus` & `ContactCard`:** Self-service application inquiry tracker and departmental telephone directory.
10. **`UniversityFooter`:** Comprehensive 4-column footer with mandatory disclosures, UGC notices, emergency helplines, campus address, and copyright.
